Method and limitations
  1. Collapsed the CTA stop list to one point per station (144 stations).
  2. Buffered stations by 2,640 ft in NAD83 / Illinois East (EPSG:3435) and dissolved.
  3. Calculated the share of each community area inside the buffer (QGIS Overlap Analysis) and counted stations per area.
  4. Joined the ACS 2008-2012 socioeconomic table on community area number.
  5. Exported the results from QGIS to GeoJSON for this map.

Walksheds are straight-line, not along streets. Coverage is by land area, not population. The hardship index (1 = least, 100 = most) is from ACS 2008-2012 and combines crowding, poverty, unemployment, education, dependency and income. Buses are not included.

Data: City of Chicago Data Portal, Chicago Transit Authority, U.S. Census Bureau. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.
